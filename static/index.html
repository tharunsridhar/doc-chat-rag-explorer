How the pipeline fits together

    

      
1
Chunk

        
Text is split into overlapping pieces small enough to embed meaningfully.

      
→

      
2
Embed

        
A local model turns each chunk into a vector that captures its meaning.

      
→

      
3
Store

        
Vectors go into a per-session Chroma collection.

      
→

      
4
Retrieve

        
The question is embedded and the closest chunks come back. No LLM yet.

      
→

      
5
Augment

        
Chunks are stitched into a prompt next to the question.

      
→

      
6
Generate

        
Groq answers from that prompt only.
